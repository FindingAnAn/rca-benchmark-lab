# Public data attribution

RCAEval: Luan Pham and RCAEval contributors. Source dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/phamquiluan/RCAEval ; code and publication links: https://github.com/phamquiluan/RCAEval . The included resource-metric subset is derived from the 15 RE1-OB CPU cases listed in its download/conversion manifests. Dataset card declares MIT. This lab is not an official RCAEval distribution or official algorithm reproduction.

TelecomTS: Ali Maatouk and TelecomTS contributors. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/AliMaatouk/TelecomTS . The included JSONL files are the first 48 windows of each of six specified captures, not the complete dataset. Dataset card declares MIT.

Pinned source cards are included in `docs/upstream/`; full origin URLs, revisions, hashes, selection and transformations are recorded with each input. No LEMMA/GAIA/AIOps/Alibaba raw corpus is redistributed. Fixtures were generated by this lab and must not be attributed as original public dataset samples.

NumPy and optional PyArrow are external runtime dependencies. Their binaries are not bundled in this archive. Preserve the licenses supplied with any wheels brought into the company environment.
